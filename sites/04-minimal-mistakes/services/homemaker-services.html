Core service

Homemaker & light housekeeping

A safer, calmer home: meals, laundry, dishes, and clutter control.

Homemaker services keep the household running when housekeeping has become physically difficult or emotionally overwhelming. Light housekeeping only — no heavy cleaning, lifting, or hazardous work.

Who it is for

Older adults whose home is becoming hard to maintain; families preparing a home for a return from the hospital.

The problem it solves

Unsafe clutter, spoiled food, laundry piles, fall hazards, and a home that no longer feels like home.

What a visit includes

  • Meal preparation and gentle nutrition reminders
  • Laundry, linens, dishes, and trash
  • Light tidying of paths, bathrooms, and kitchen surfaces
  • Grocery list building and errand support
  • Decluttering focused on fall prevention, not deep cleaning

How we quote this service

Every service is quoted in writing after a no-cost conversation. The quote itemizes hours, any weekend, overnight, or holiday premium, and travel, and it does not change without your written agreement. There is no assessment fee and no long-term contract.

Scope and supervision

Professional care only

Heart & Harbor Medical Support provides professional home care. We do not provide skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, wound care, or medication administration. Personal care requires a Virginia home care organization license; this concept site does not claim an active license.
