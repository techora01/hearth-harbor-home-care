What is included

Caregiver wages and payroll taxes, supervision, the written care plan, scheduling, background checks, routine family updates, and ordinary supplies used during the visit.

What costs extra

Holiday visits, awake overnight shifts, mileage for transportation, event tickets, and specialized supplies. Every premium is disclosed before the visit is scheduled.

How to cancel

Seven days' written notice to end service. Twenty-four hours' notice to cancel an individual visit without charge, except in an emergency.

Ways families pay

Private pay and long-term care insurance

Most non-medical care is paid privately. If a long-term care policy covers home care, we can provide the documentation the insurer requires — care notes, invoices, and a plan of care — but reimbursement is never guaranteed.

Veterans' benefits

Some wartime veterans and surviving spouses qualify for VA aid and attendance or housebound benefits. Eligibility is determined by the VA, not by us. We can help assemble the service documentation.

Medicaid and Medicare

Medicare generally covers short-term skilled home health ordered by a physician, not custodial or companion care. Virginia's CCC Plus waiver can cover personal care and respite for eligible adults through enrolled providers. This concept business is not enrolled and makes no coverage claim.

Questions

What families ask first.

Are you licensed?

This is a concept website. Hearth & Harbor Home Care does not yet hold a Virginia home care organization license. Personal care services in Virginia require licensure by the Virginia Department of Health, and the launch site must display the actual license number once issued. Companion and homemaker services can be offered without an HCO license when provided alone, but this business intends to be licensed before delivering personal care.

What is the difference between home care and home health?

Home care is non-medical help with daily living — companionship, meals, housekeeping, transportation, and personal care such as bathing and dressing. Home health is skilled clinical care ordered by a physician, such as nursing, physical therapy, or occupational therapy, and is delivered by a Medicare-certified home health agency. We provide home care and refer clinical needs to licensed providers.

How much does care cost?

Rates on this site are illustrative placeholders for a Prince William County launch. Local market data suggests roughly $35–$42 per hour for non-medical care. We publish a flat hourly rate, disclose holiday and overnight premiums, require a four-hour minimum visit, and do not require a long-term contract.

Do you accept insurance, Medicare, or Medicaid?

No payment relationships are claimed on this concept site. Most non-medical home care in Virginia is paid privately, through long-term care insurance, or through veterans' benefits. Medicaid personal care is available through the CCC Plus waiver but requires DMAS enrollment and a provider agreement. Confirm coverage with the agency before making a decision.

Can you give my mother her medication?

Caregivers can remind, prompt, and document. Medication administration is a regulated nursing task and is outside our scope. We coordinate with the prescriber, pharmacy, and family, and we use a written medication list approved by the family.

What happens if our caregiver is sick?

Every client is assigned a named backup caregiver who has been introduced before an emergency. If both are unavailable, the coordinator covers the visit or arranges a qualified substitute. You receive a call, not a surprise.
