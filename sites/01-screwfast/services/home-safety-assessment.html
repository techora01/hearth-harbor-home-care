Phase 2 service

Home safety & aging-in-place review

A room-by-room look at falls, lighting, bathrooms, and exits.

A practical walkthrough with a written report the family can act on. Recommendations focus on low-cost fixes first, with contractor referrals only where the family asks.

Who it is for

Families planning for the next five years, or reacting to a first fall.

The problem it solves

Most falls happen at home, and most hazards are cheap to fix but easy to overlook.

What a visit includes

  • Room-by-room hazard checklist
  • Bathroom, stair, and lighting review
  • Emergency exit and phone-access check
  • Written prioritized action list with cost ranges
  • Optional 30-day follow-up to confirm fixes

What it costs

Flat assessment fee, credited toward the first month of care. See the full pricing page for illustrative rates and the minimum-visit policy.

Regulatory notes

Virginia scope and supervision

Non-clinical. Do not perform construction, electrical, or medical work. Use licensed contractors for modifications.

Business case

A paid entry offer that demonstrates expertise before a care commitment.
