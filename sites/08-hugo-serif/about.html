Our approach

A smaller agency, built for the parts of care that get skipped.

Heart & Harbor Medical Support is a concept for a licensed, locally owned home care organization in Prince William County. The operating model is deliberately narrow: fewer clients, named coordinators, and written communication.

The problem with “care” as a category

Home care is sold with the same vocabulary everywhere: compassionate, trusted, family-first. Those words are not wrong. They are just not operational. A family cannot tell from a brochure whether the caregiver will arrive at 8:05 or 8:40, whether the coordinator knows the medication list, or whether anyone will call when something changes.

We are designing around the operational questions instead.

The six-step process

01

A 15-minute call

Tell us what is happening. We ask about safety, mobility, medications, and what the family has already tried. No sales pressure and no assessment fee.

02

In-home conversation

A coordinator visits the home, meets the client, reviews the routine, and completes a non-clinical safety walkthrough. Family can join by video.

03

A written care plan

You receive the schedule, the named caregiver, the tasks, the written quote, and the escalation contacts before care begins.

04

A deliberate match

We introduce the primary caregiver and a trained backup. We check fit at 72 hours and again at 14 days, and change the match if it is not working.

05

Weekly communication

The Friday Family Brief summarizes the week. The coordinator answers questions during office hours; the on-call line handles evenings and weekends.

06

Review every 90 days

Needs change. We revisit the plan, the hours, and the cost on a scheduled cadence — and sooner if something changes.

What we will not do

  • We will not provide skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, wound care, or medication administration.
  • We will not advertise a license, insurance panel, or credential we do not hold.
  • We will not use invented testimonials or stock “caregiver of the month” stories.
  • We will not require a long-term contract to start care.
  • We will not promise 24-hour coverage before the staffing model can support it.

Who is behind it

This is a launch concept, not an operating agency. The site intentionally avoids naming staff, credentials, or clinical leadership until those people are hired and verified. A real launch requires a qualified administrator, a supervising nurse for personal care, documented training, background checks, insurance, and a VDH license.

In writing

What we put in the service agreement.

No long-term contract

Seven days' written notice to pause or end service. No cancellation penalty for illness or hospitalization.

A written quote

The rate in your signed care agreement is the rate we bill. Weekend, overnight, and mileage terms are disclosed in writing before the first visit.

A named backup

Every client has a trained backup caregiver who has been introduced before an emergency.

Background checks before contact

Virginia State Police criminal record check and sworn disclosure before any direct client contact, consistent with 12VAC5-381-110.

No clinical overreach

We do not provide skilled nursing, physical therapy, wound care, or medication administration. We refer to licensed providers.

Privacy by design

Care notes are shared only with people the client authorizes. Photos require written consent.
