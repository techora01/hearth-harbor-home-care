Commitment

Accessibility

The site is designed to be usable by older adults, family caregivers, and people using assistive technology.

What we have built in

  • Semantic landmarks, a skip link, and a visible keyboard focus style
  • Text contrast targets aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA where the visual system allows
  • Responsive typography and touch targets of at least 44 pixels for primary controls
  • Reduced-motion support and no autoplaying video or audio
  • Form labels, error messaging, and a status region for screen readers
  • Alternative text for meaningful imagery and decorative SVGs hidden from assistive tech

Known limitations

This is a demonstration build. It has not yet had an independent accessibility audit or usability testing with older adults. Some decorative illustrations are complex; they are marked decorative, with the meaning carried in adjacent text. The Google Fonts dependency can be removed for a fully self-hosted deployment.
