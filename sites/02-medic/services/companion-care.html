Companion care

Conversation, companionship, walks, hobbies, and a dependable presence.

Companion care is for older adults who are safe with daily tasks but spend too much time alone. It is also the lowest-risk way for a family to test whether outside help feels right.

Who it is for

Older adults living alone, recently widowed, or isolated after a move; families who live out of state.

The problem it solves

Loneliness, skipped meals, missed social contact, and adult children worrying through the workday.

What a visit includes

  • Friendly visits, conversation, cards, reading, and shared hobbies
  • Walks, porch time, and encouragement to attend senior center programs
  • Meal planning and simple meal preparation together
  • Grocery lists, errands, and appointment companionship
  • Family check-in notes after every visit

What it costs

Hourly, 4-hour minimum; weekly packages of 8, 12, or 20 hours. See the full pricing page for illustrative rates and the minimum-visit policy.

Regulatory notes

Virginia scope and supervision

Companion services are exempt from Virginia home care licensure when provided alone, but an organization providing personal care needs a VDH home care organization license. This concept is designed to operate under the licensed model.

Business case

Recurring weekday visits create a predictable revenue base and a natural path to personal care as needs change.
