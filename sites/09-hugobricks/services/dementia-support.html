Dementia-friendly support

Routine, redirection, and a calm environment for memory changes.

Dementia support is non-clinical care grounded in routine, validation, and environmental calm. It is appropriate for families who need consistent hands-on help and coaching, not a diagnosis or a cure.

Who it is for

Families navigating early- to mid-stage dementia who want to stay home longer.

The problem it solves

Wandering, sundowning, repetitive questions, resistance to bathing, and caregiver exhaustion.

What a visit includes

  • Consistent caregiver assignment and a predictable daily rhythm
  • Redirection and validation techniques, not confrontation
  • Meal, hydration, and bathroom prompts
  • Home safety review for wandering and night-time risk
  • Family coaching and written escalation guidance

What it costs

Premium hourly rate; 6-hour minimum for dementia visits. See the full pricing page for illustrative rates and the minimum-visit policy.

Regulatory notes

Virginia scope and supervision

Non-clinical only. Staff need specialized dementia training and supervision. Do not market memory care, nursing, or behavioral treatment. Wandering risk may require a higher level of care.

Business case

Premium rate, high loyalty, and strong word-of-mouth in caregiver communities.
