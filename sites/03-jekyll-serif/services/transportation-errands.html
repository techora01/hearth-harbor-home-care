Core service

Transportation & errands

Rides to appointments, groceries, pharmacy, and church — with a helper.

Transportation is one of the most requested and least reliable services for older adults in suburban Prince William County, where driving is often the difference between independence and isolation.

Who it is for

Adults who no longer drive confidently, especially in Woodbridge, Gainesville, and Haymarket.

The problem it solves

Missed appointments, skipped prescriptions, and isolation when family schedules do not align.

What a visit includes

  • Door-through-door accompaniment, not a curb drop-off
  • Medical, dental, pharmacy, grocery, and worship trips
  • Waiting-room companionship when requested
  • Errand running with receipts and a written summary
  • Coordination with family calendars

How we quote this service

Every service is quoted in writing after a no-cost conversation. The quote itemizes hours, any weekend, overnight, or holiday premium, and travel, and it does not change without your written agreement. There is no assessment fee and no long-term contract.

Scope and supervision

Professional care only

Heart & Harbor Medical Support provides professional home care. We do not provide skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, wound care, or medication administration. Personal care requires a Virginia home care organization license; this concept site does not claim an active license.
