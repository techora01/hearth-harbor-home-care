For discharge planners & case managers

Non-clinical support for the first 72 hours home.

Medicare covers short-term skilled home health. It generally does not cover the practical, non-clinical help a patient needs in the first days home. We fill that gap — and we do not compete with your clinical referrals.

What the bridge includes

  • Ride home from the facility
  • Pharmacy pickup and grocery run
  • Meal setup and hydration check
  • Fall-hazard clearing along the path from door to bed to bath
  • Medication reminder setup (reminders only)
  • 24-hour and 72-hour check-in calls
  • Written summary to the family and the referring planner

Response standard

Same-day triage during business hours. Written scope confirmation before the first visit. One intake number, answered by a coordinator — not a call center.

What we do not do

  • × No skilled nursing or wound care
  • × No physical, occupational, or speech therapy
  • × No medication administration
  • × No IV therapy or clinical monitoring
  • × No change to the home health plan of care

Coordination, not duplication

We ask for the discharge instructions and the home health agency's contact so our non-clinical tasks stay inside the clinical plan. If a patient needs skilled care, we say so and step back.

Who we serve

Adults discharging home who need non-clinical support; older adults aging in place; family caregivers who need respite; patients whose families live out of state.

What we need from a referral

Patient name and preferred contact · discharge date and time · non-clinical needs · language preference · who is authorized to receive updates · the home health agency's name, if one is involved. Please do not send clinical records through email; we will use a secure channel.

Compliance note: Heart & Harbor Homecare is a concept for a Virginia-licensed home care organization. No license is currently claimed. We do not pay for referrals and do not accept per-referral compensation. Personal care requires a Virginia HCO license and clinical supervision.
