Scope, stated plainly

What we do not do

Heart & Harbor Health & Medical Support is a professional home care agency. We do not provide:

  • Skilled nursing or wound care
  • Physical, occupational, or speech therapy
  • Medication administration
  • IV therapy or clinical monitoring
  • Hospice or memory care

We work alongside the clinical team — the home health nurse, the physician, the therapist — rather than replacing it.

Why we say this first

Families are used to being oversold. Knowing exactly where our scope ends is the fastest way to trust where it begins.

When to call

When everyday tasks start to slip

Bathing, dressing, meals, errands, and appointments can become hard long before a family admits it. The first sign is rarely a diagnosis. It is a stack of unopened mail, a fridge with nothing in it, or a parent who stopped going to church.

We help with the parts of the day that have become difficult — without taking away independence.

What we do

  • Companion care and conversation
  • Personal care assistance (ADLs)
  • Respite for family caregivers
  • Homemaker and light housekeeping
  • Transportation and errands
  • Medication reminders
  • Wellness check-ins

Four hours a week is a real start

You do not have to redesign your parent's life to make it safer. A consistent four-hour visit often prevents the crisis that would.

Getting started

From first call to first visit

  1. A 15-minute call. Tell us what is happening. No assessment fee. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.
  2. A home visit. A coordinator walks the home, learns the routine, and writes a care plan. About 45–60 minutes.
  3. A caregiver introduction. You and your parent meet the primary caregiver and, where possible, the backup — before care starts.
  4. A 72-hour check-in. We call to ask whether the fit is right. If it is not, we change it.
  5. A weekly written update. Every Friday, in plain language.

What it costs

Companion care from $32/hour; personal care from $36/hour; respite from $34/hour. Four-hour minimum. No long-term contract.
