Printing notes Print at 100% scale (do not "fit to page") for accurate card and tri-fold dimensions. Enable background graphics. For the tri-fold: print double-sided, flip on the short edge, then fold in thirds with the front cover panel on the outside. For the cards: print page 1, then page 2 on the reverse, and trim on the light borders. Colors are RGB; a professional printer may ask for CMYK conversion.
