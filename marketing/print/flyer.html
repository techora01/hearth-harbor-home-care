Is your parent safe at home?

A 10-minute conversation can help. No sales pitch, no assessment fee — just plain answers about what is normal, what is not, and what to do next.

Five signs it may be time for help

  1. The fridge is empty, or food has gone bad.
  2. Mail is piled up and bills are unopened.
  3. They have stopped going to church, the store, or the salon.
  4. There is a new bruise, or a story about a fall that does not quite add up.
  5. They seem thinner, more tired, or more confused than three months ago.

One sign is a question. Three is a pattern.

You do not need a diagnosis to start a conversation. You need a Tuesday afternoon and a little honesty.
