# Source Register

**Compiled:** 23 November 2026
**Purpose:** Every factual claim in the strategy library should be traceable to one of these sources.
**Access note:** Sources marked **Reviewed** were read directly during research. Sources marked **Identified** appeared in search results or agency directories and should be re-verified before being cited publicly. All URLs are provided for verification; content can change.

> **Citation discipline.** Where a figure is an estimate, a range, or a planning assumption, the document says so. Do not upgrade an estimate to a fact because it appears in a table. Regulations are especially prone to change; confirm against the current Virginia Administrative Code before filing.

## 8. Citation format used in this library

In-text citations use bracketed IDs: `[S6]`, `[S22]`.

When a claim is an estimate, the text says **Assumption** or **Planning estimate**. When a claim is a recommendation, it says **Recommendation**.

**Do not** cite this library as a primary source in a legal filing or an investor deck without returning to the underlying source.
