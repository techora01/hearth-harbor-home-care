# SEO & Keyword Strategy — Prince William County Home Care

**Prepared:** 23 November 2026
**Scope:** Organic search, local pack, and AI-answer visibility for a new non-medical home care agency.
**Domain assumption:** `hearthandharborcare.com` (placeholder — verify availability).

> **Data honesty.** No paid keyword tool was used for this concept. Search volumes below are **planning estimates** based on the structure of local service search, not measured data. Replace them with Ahrefs, Semrush, or Google Keyword Planner data before committing budget.

---

## 1. SEO thesis

Local home care search is:

1. **Urgent and mobile.** Most queries happen during or after a crisis, on a phone.
2. **Geo-modified.** "Home care Woodbridge VA" converts far better than "home care."
3. **Trust-gated.** Searchers compare 2–4 agencies; rates, reviews, and a real local address decide.
4. **Fragmenting into AI answers.** Google's AI overviews and chat assistants increasingly answer informational queries, so the site must be citable, structured, and specific.

**Strategy:** own the intersection of **service × community × intent**, publish genuinely useful local content, and make every page technically clean and schema-rich.

## 4. On-page standards

Every page must have:

- One `<h1>` that matches search intent.
- A title tag under 60 characters with the primary keyword and brand.
- A meta description of 140–160 characters with a concrete benefit and a CTA.
- A canonical URL.
- Open Graph and Twitter card metadata.
- JSON-LD: `HomeHealthCareService` sitewide; `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages; `FAQPage` on the homepage and pricing; `Service` on service pages; `Article` on guides.
- Descriptive alt text on meaningful images; decorative SVGs marked `aria-hidden`.
- Internal links to the parent hub, two sibling pages, and one conversion page.
- A visible last-updated date on evergreen guides.

**Heading discipline:** H2 for major sections, H3 for sub-points. Never skip levels for styling.

## 5. Local SEO

### 5.1 Google Business Profile

- Primary category: **Home Health Care Service**
- Secondary categories only if accurate: Home Health Care Agency? (only if licensed), Companion Care? (if available)
- Service area: Prince William County and named communities; hide the street address if clients do not visit the office.
- Hours: match the published hours; mark after-hours as appointment-only.
- Attributes: LGBTQ+ friendly, veteran-led (only if true), languages spoken (only if staffed).
- Posts: weekly, educational.
- Photos: office, team (with consent), training, local landmarks — 10+.
- Q&A: seed with real questions and answer them.
- Reviews: request from every satisfied client with written consent; never incentivize.

### 5.2 Citations

Consistent NAP on: Google, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Nextdoor, Facebook, LinkedIn, Better Business Bureau, local chamber of commerce, Virginia home care association directories, and A Place for Mom/Caring.com (if the economics work).

### 5.3 Reviews

- Target 20+ verified reviews in year one.
- Ask at the 90-day mark, when value is proven.
- Respond to every review within 48 hours.
- Never publish a review without consent.
- Never write a review on a client's behalf.

## 7. Technical SEO checklist

- [ ] Static HTML, fast first paint, no render-blocking JS.
- [ ] Mobile-first responsive layout; 44px touch targets.
- [ ] HTTPS; HSTS; canonical host.
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted; robots.txt correct.
- [ ] Clean URLs; no duplicate content.
- [ ] 404 page with navigation.
- [ ] Structured data validated (Rich Results Test).
- [ ] Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms.
- [ ] Fonts self-hosted or preconnected; no layout shift.
- [ ] Images compressed; SVG where possible.
- [ ] Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA target.
- [ ] Internal link audit: no orphans.
- [ ] Redirect map from any old domain.
- [ ] Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster verified.
- [ ] Analytics installed with consent (and HIPAA review — do not send PHI to analytics).

**Privacy note:** standard analytics can capture form field contents and URLs. Configure to exclude query parameters that might contain health information, and never place a pixel on a form confirmation page that includes PHI.

## 8. AI search visibility

More families now ask an assistant "what home care agencies serve Woodbridge VA" or "does Medicare cover home care." To be cited:

1. **Answer the question in the first 60 words** of each guide.
2. **Use clear headings that match the question.**
3. **Include a short, quotable summary** ("Bottom line") on every article.
4. **Cite primary sources** (Medicare.gov, Virginia code) with links.
5. **Keep facts current** with a visible date.
6. **Use schema** (`FAQPage`, `Article`, `Service`).
7. **Be specific about place** — name the county, cities, ZIPs, and hospitals.
8. **Avoid vague marketing language** that an AI cannot quote.

## 10. What not to do

- **Do not buy links.** Local trust is fragile.
- **Do not stuff city names** into every paragraph.
- **Do not publish thin location pages** with swapped city names only.
- **Do not target "24 hour care"** before the staffing exists.
- **Do not target "nursing" or "medical home care"** — wrong license, wrong intent.
- **Do not use fake reviews** or review gating that violates platform rules.
- **Do not ignore the Google Business Profile.** For local service search, it often outranks the website.
