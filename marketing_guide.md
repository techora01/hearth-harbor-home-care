
# Logo Concept Description
A modern, professional logo for a home healthcare business in Prince William County, VA. Use calming colors like blue and green. Incorporate subtle medical or care symbols. Clean, minimalist design suitable for a website header.

**Suggested Design Elements:**
*   **Iconography:** A subtle abstract shape combining a house outline (home) with a heart or a gentle, flowing curve (care/health).
*   **Color Palette:** Primary: Deep Teal/Navy Blue (#004D66), Secondary: Soft Sage Green (#8FBC8F), Accent: Light Grey or Cream.
*   **Font Pairing:** Clean sans-serif for headings (e.g., Montserrat, Poppins) and a readable serif for body text if desired.
